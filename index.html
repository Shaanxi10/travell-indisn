Before you leave

Simple habits that reduce avoidable travel problems.

📱

Keep your phone ready

Charge your phone, keep a power bank, and store your booking details offline.

👥

Share your plan

Send your hotel, transport and approximate itinerary to a trusted person.

🪪

Protect documents

Carry necessary ID securely and keep digital copies in a protected account.

🏨

Check your stay

Confirm the property name, address, check-in process and contact number before arrival.

🚆

Transport awareness

Confirm the operator, route and pickup point. Avoid handing over valuables unnecessarily.

🌙

Late-night planning

Prefer well-lit public areas, trusted transport and known accommodation after dark.

Quick safety checklist

Useful reminders you can follow during a trip.

01Keep emergency contact details and hotel information easy to access.
02Use official or trusted transport providers whenever possible.
03Do not share OTPs, passwords, card PINs or sensitive account information.
04If you feel unsafe, move toward a populated, well-lit place and seek official help.
05For an immediate emergency in India, use the official 112 emergency response service.
Emergency information: Government of India ERSS / 112. Source: https://112.gov.in/ and https://www.mha.gov.in/en/commoncontent/emergency-response-support-system-erss
